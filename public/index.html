What it measures

Who you believeGround patrol, drone feed and signals intercept disagree about the same ground on purpose. You decide which is right.
How sure you were, and whether you should have beenEvery call carries a confidence. Saying 90% and being right half the time is scored as the failure it is.
An opponent that learns youLean on the drone and it spoofs the drone. Trust the patrol's "all clear" and the patrol goes stale. Your record follows your name between rounds.
Proof you got betterCalibration round by round, from real play, with the sample size shown beside every figure.
Cost of fogFor every threat, when a commander could have known, against when they did. The gap is the headline.
Casualties taken blindLosses to enemies that were not on the commander's map when contact began, kept separate from losses taken with eyes open.
How deception was handledPhantom contacts, forged orders and false kill reports, each traced from injection to whether it was believed, checked or refused.
Mission commandWhen a commander is cut off, do they keep acting on the commander's intent, or wait for orders that are never coming?
The shared pictureHow far the three commanders' maps drifted apart, second by second.
The red cell's judgementAn automated adversary that picks its own moment to jam or deceive, and writes down why it chose it.
